# Release scope and maintenance

Release 0.2 completes the English SMS research implementation: data audit, grouped partitions, two model comparisons, calibration and review policy, browser inference, versioned DataGuard, controlled monitoring experiments, authored diagnostics, automated checks and a professional final technical report.

The scope is a text-based investigation aid. It does not include automatic blocking, URL reputation lookup, images, voice, other languages, automatic retraining, a central database or a presentation. No claim of future accuracy through 2030 or production-grade fraud prevention is established.

Future promotion requires new governed English data, fresh evaluation and a matching model/reference version. The current report records EWMA failures and the conservative-bound tradeoff; it does not claim a novel adaptive detector or independent usability study.

Installation and reproduction are in README.md and ml/MODEL_V2.md. Results and limitations are in docs/FINAL_REPORT.md. docs/HISTORY_V1.md contains superseded first-release notes, not current instructions.
